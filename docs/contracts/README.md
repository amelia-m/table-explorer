# Task contracts

What an agent was asked to build, agreed with Amelia before the work started.
Kept here rather than in `_scratch/` so the reasoning survives the session and
travels with the repository: a contract says what was in scope, what was
deliberately excluded, which thresholds were chosen and why, and what evidence
the work had to produce.

A draft carries `-draft` in its name and has open questions at the bottom. A
contract without that suffix was approved and dispatched; its "Settled"
section records the decisions and who made them.
